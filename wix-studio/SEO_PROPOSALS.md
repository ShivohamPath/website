## 4. Not changed, and why

- Cart, search, thank-you, programme list and the old `/book-online` page are
  already hidden from Google (correct).
- Homepage, Readings, About, Courses, Lineage, Contact and The Secret Side of
  Jyotish already have good custom titles and descriptions.
- Most other blog posts have descriptions written automatically by Wix
  ("Explore…", "Discover…"). They work, but rewriting them in your voice
  would help clicks; that's a bigger, optional job.
