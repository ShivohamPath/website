A Note from Veena · Day 3

The moment I stopped reading charts the old way.

I used to read charts the way most people teach it. Planets in houses, dashas, predictions. Someone would come to me and I'd tell them: this year looks difficult, this transit brings opportunity, marriage is likely in this window.

And people would leave satisfied. But I would leave feeling like I had given them something hollow.

Because the chart wasn't asking me what would happen to them. It was asking me why they had chosen this birth. What karma had shaped this soul before it arrived here. What it came to face, to resolve, to finally release.

That is what Parashara actually wrote about. Not "when will I get a job." The Karmavipak Samhita, the Brhat Nakshatra, the classical nadi texts — they are not prediction manuals. They are karma manuals. The planets are not causes. They are karmic signatures.

The moment I made that shift — and started reading charts through that lens — everything changed. Sessions became something different. People didn't just leave with information. They left with recognition. The kind that lands in the body.

"Your patterns are not personality.
They are karma asking to be seen."

That's what I want to teach. Not astrology as a parlour trick. Not predictions. But Jyotish as it was always meant to be understood — a living map of the soul's karma, held in light.

 

I'm building something for people like you.
I'll tell you more very soon.

With love,

Veena
