Course Access Guide
     

How to log in and find your course.

If you're having trouble getting into your course, you're not alone — and it's simpler than it looks. Follow the steps below exactly, in order, and you'll be inside in under two minutes.

Step 1

Open the website

Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox — whichever you normally use) and go to:

www.shivohampath.com

Step 2

Find the Login button

The login button is in different places depending on your device:

On your phone: Look at the very top of the page — there is a thin gold bar that says LOGIN. Tap it.

On your computer: Scroll all the way to the bottom of any page. In the footer, under the column that says "My Account", click Login / Sign Up ★.

Step 3

Sign in with your email and password

A login window will appear. Enter the email address and password you used when you enrolled. Then click Log In.

First time here? Click Sign Up instead and create a free account using the same email address you received this message on. This connects you to your enrollment automatically.

Step 4

Go to your course

Once logged in, click My Account (same place the Login button was). You'll land on your account page. Look for the section called My Programmes or My Challenges — your course will be listed there. Click on it to enter.

You can also use the button below — it will take you straight to the login page.

Go to My Account →

Forgotten your password? On the login screen, click Forgot Password and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it in your inbox.

 

If you have followed all four steps and still cannot get in, simply reply to this email with a screenshot of where you're stuck. I'll sort it out personally.
