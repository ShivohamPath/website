Madhurya Bhakti

The Path of Loving Shiva as Beloved — Not as Deity

Madhurya Bhakti is perhaps the most misunderstood of all the devotional paths. It is not worship. It is not prayer. It is the radical dissolution of the boundary between the one who loves and the one who is loved — until only love remains. This is where this body of courses begins.

THIS COURSE COVERS
  • What Madhurya Bhakti actually is — and what it is not
  • The relationship between bhakti and non-dual Shaivism
  • Working with longing as a spiritual instrument
  • The lived practice of divine union in ordinary life
