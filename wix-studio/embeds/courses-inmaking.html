Śivo'ham — The Recognition

An advanced course in Kashmir Shaivism for women · Successor to She Who Becomes Him

She Who Becomes Him closed the distance between she and Him — longing, burning, surrender, union. This course removes the bridge itself. In Kashmir Shaivism there is no gap to cross: what feels like distance is only the contraction that makes you experience yourself as small, separate, incomplete. Nothing must be attained. Something must be recognised. Union was the ceiling of the first course. It is the floor of this one.

Seven modules through the three upāyas — body, mind, will — into grace and liberation while living. Rooted in the Śiva Sūtras, the Vijñāna Bhairava, the Spanda Kārikās, and the vākhs of Lalleshwari.
