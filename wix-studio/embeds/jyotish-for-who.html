WHO THIS IS FOR

This Course Is For
Seekers, Not Hobbyists

You don't need to have studied astrology before. You don't need to know a single planet. What you need is the genuine desire to understand — not just the charts, but the karmic reality that the charts are pointing to.

✦
The Karma Seeker You've always felt that astrology has to be about more than predictions. You sense that your chart holds something about why you are here — and you want to learn to read it.
✦
The Complete Beginner You know nothing about Jyotish and want to start from the actual foundation — not YouTube videos and free apps, but a systematic, classical understanding built from the ground up.
✦
The Experienced Student Who Feels Lost You've studied Jyotish elsewhere and something still doesn't click. You can place planets but you can't read a story. This course fills in what was missing — the karmic layer beneath the technique.
✦
The Bhakti Practitioner or Spiritual Seeker Your path is devotional or contemplative and you want to understand what your chart says about your dharma, your karma, and your soul's movement toward liberation in this lifetime.
