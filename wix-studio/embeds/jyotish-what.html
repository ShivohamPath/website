WHAT THIS COURSE IS

Jyotish Was Always
About One Thing Only

"The planets do not cause your life.
They reveal the karma that is shaping it."

Parashara did not give us the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra to predict who would be rich. He gave it to help souls understand why they chose this birth: what contracts they carry, what they came to complete, and what this lifetime is truly asking of them.

That is the Jyotish this course teaches. Not the astrology of events. The astrology of karma.

ROOTED IN THE CLASSICAL TEXTS
Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra The foundational scripture of Jyotish, authored by Maharishi Parashara. The complete architecture of karmic chart reading.
Brhat Nakshatra The definitive classical text on the 27 Nakshatras: the soul's deepest emotional and karmic imprints encoded in the lunar mansions.
Karmavipak Samhita A rare classical text dealing directly with the results of karma: what past actions create as present circumstances, and how the chart carries this record.
Classical Nadi Texts The ancient oral tradition of soul-level reading, where specific planetary combinations reveal past-life origins and the soul's unfinished work.
