THE TEACHING

The Eye of the Vedas

Most people come to Jyotish with one question.

"What is going to happen to me?"

It is a human question. Completely understandable. But it is not what this science was designed to answer.

Parasara, the sage who gave us Jyotish, opens his teaching with a cosmological statement. The Lord himself incarnated as the nine planets specifically to deliver to each living being the fruits of its own karma. Not to forecast. Not to warn. To complete. To move each soul toward whatever it still needs to learn and resolve.

This changes everything about how you read a chart.

The birth chart is not a map of events waiting to happen. It is a map of karma, the accumulated soul-history your jeevatma brought into this lifetime, and the portion of that karma now ripe for living. Once you see it this way, the chart stops being a source of anxiety and becomes something far more useful.

A map of your own curriculum.

SANSKRIT SLOKA · BPHS VOL. 1, CH. 2, SLOKAS 3-4

Karmaphala praday' aiva Nava Graharupinam Bhagavan Janardanah Avatara chakre prabhuh

"The unborn Lord, to bestow on all living beings the fruits due to their karmas, has incarnated in the form of the nine planets. He is Janardana, the one who moves beings toward their karma."

Parasara to Maitreya

The planets are not mechanical celestial objects. They are conscious divine agents, each carrying specific karmic instruction for your soul. When you look at your chart through this lens, everything shifts.

THE THREE KARMAS

Your Chart Has Three Layers

Each visible in a different part of the birth chart. Each asking a different question of your soul.

Sanchita

ACCUMULATED KARMA

The entire storehouse of karma across all past lifetimes. Like a vast cosmic library, every thought, action, and intention your soul has ever generated across every incarnation. You cannot clear it all in one lifetime. But you can see its general texture in the chart as a whole.

In your chart: The whole birth chart. Especially Ketu (past-life mastery) and the D60 (Shastiamsha). The overall atmosphere of your chart, its density and dominant themes, speaks of Sanchita.

Prarabdha

ACTIVATED KARMA

The portion of Sanchita karma ripe for this lifetime, already set in motion at birth. The books placed on your desk. You cannot put them back. This is why your soul chose this particular body, family, and circumstances. It is not punishment. It is curriculum.

In your chart: Most visible in your Lagna (1st house), 5th house, and 9th house, the Dharma Trikona. Also in the Vimshottari Dasha sequence, which maps the timing of when specific karma activates.

Kriyamana

BEING CREATED NOW

The karma you are actively creating right now through every choice, thought, and conscious action in this lifetime. This is the domain of free will. Every aware response to your circumstances either deepens old patterns or begins to dissolve them.

In your chart: No fixed house, because Kriyamana is not a placement. It is what you do with every placement. The act of studying Jyotish is itself Kriyamana karma. Awareness changes the trajectory.

A chart reading prompt · Have your birth chart open as you read these questions.

01 · SANCHITA

Look at your whole chart without any technical knowledge. What is the general atmosphere? Concentrated or spread out? Heavy or light? What does your first impression reveal about what this soul has been carrying?

02 · PRARABDHA

Find your Lagna (1st house), 5th house, and 9th house, the Dharma Trikona. Without interpreting technically, what qualities do these signs carry? What story do they suggest about why you are here this lifetime?

03 · KRIYAMANA

You are reading this right now. That is not random. Write down one question you want to understand about your own chart. That question is your Kriyamana karma beginning to move.
