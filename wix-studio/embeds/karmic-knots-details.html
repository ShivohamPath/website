The Three Charts

The Karmic Knots Reading integrates three distinct charts, each revealing a different layer of your soul's journey. Together, they create a complete picture of your karmic architecture.

D1

Rashi

This Lifetime

Your birth chart. The planetary positions at your exact moment of birth. This is the surface — what you experience, the events, the circumstances, the personality you show the world.

D9

Navamsha

Soul's Dharma

Your soul's purpose. This chart reveals what your soul came here to express, the gifts it carries, the dharma it must fulfill. It shows your highest calling in this lifetime.

D60

Shashtiamsha

All Lifetimes · All Karma

The soul's complete history. Every past life, every karmic debt, every unresolved wound, every karmic gift. This is the deepest layer — the why beneath the what.

KARMIC ROOT

The Pattern Beneath

Every confusing life pattern — the relationships that repeat, the fears that persist, the patterns you cannot break — has a root in your karmic architecture. This reading uncovers that root, showing you why these patterns exist and what they are trying to teach you.

SOUL CONTRACTS

Past-Life Wounds

You carry wounds from lifetimes your conscious mind doesn't remember. These wounds shape your present relationships, your triggers, your deepest vulnerabilities. This reading identifies them, showing you where they came from and how to heal them.

KARMIC GIFTS

Your Soul's Wealth

Your soul has earned gifts across multiple lifetimes — strengths, intuitions, talents, abilities. Most people never access these gifts because they don't know they carry them. This reading illuminates them, showing you what you already own.

THE MISSION

Why You're Here

This lifetime is not random. Your soul came into this particular time, in this particular circumstance, to resolve something specific. This reading reveals what your soul came to complete and what happens when you finally do.
