THE READING

What Is the
Karmic Knots Reading?

Most people come to astrology looking for predictions. This reading does something different. It looks underneath — at the karmic structure your soul built across lifetimes — to find the root of what is happening in your life right now.

A Karmic Knots Reading integrates three charts from Jyotish: the D1, which maps this lifetime; the D9, which reveals your soul's dharma; and the D60 Shashtiamsha — the rarest and most precise chart in Vedic astrology, encoding every past life your soul has carried. Together, they create a complete picture of why you are the way you are, why certain patterns repeat, and what your soul actually came here to do.

This is not interpretation. Veena reads what is literally written in your chart — through the lens of classical Jyotish texts — and speaks plainly about what she sees. No generalizations, no guesswork. What the chart holds, the reading delivers.

THIS READING IS FOR YOU IF
  • You keep repeating the same patterns in relationships, work, or inner life — and cannot understand why
  • You carry something you cannot name — a weight, a wound, a fear with no clear origin
  • You have tried therapy, healing, shadow work — and still feel something deeper is unresolved
  • You want to understand your soul's actual purpose, not a generic sun-sign reading
  • You are at a crossroads and want clarity that goes beyond logic or circumstance
  • You are drawn to past lives and want to see what your chart actually says — not speculation
