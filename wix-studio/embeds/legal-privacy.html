SHIVOHAM PATH

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 14 AUGUST 2026

This policy explains what personal information Shivoham Path collects when you visit this website, subscribe to the newsletter, write to us, book a reading, or enrol in a course — and what we do with it. We collect as little as the work requires, and we do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

01Who We Are

Shivoham Path ("we", "us") publishes teachings, courses, and readings on Jyotish, Kashmir Shaivism, and the esoteric traditions, and operates the website at shivohampath.com. For anything relating to your privacy, write to info@shivohampath.com.

We are based in India, and this website is operated from there.

02What We Collect

INFORMATION YOU GIVE US

  • Newsletter signup: your email address.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, the type of enquiry, and the message you write.
  • Bookings and readings: the details you provide when booking — which, for a chart reading, ordinarily includes your date, time, and place of birth.
  • Course enrolment: your name, email address, and account details for the members area.
  • Anything you choose to send us by email, Instagram, or in the course of a session.

INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

  • Standard technical data your browser sends — IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and the time of your visit — collected by our website host as part of normal operation and security.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, described in section 5.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

When you pay for a course or reading, your card or banking details go directly to the payment processor. We never see or store your full payment details. We receive only a confirmation that payment succeeded, along with your name and email.

On birth data: the birth details you share for a reading are treated as confidential. They are used to prepare and deliver your reading and for nothing else — never shared, sold, published, or used as an example without your explicit permission.

03How We Use It

  • To send you the newsletter and teachings you asked for.
  • To answer your enquiries and stay in correspondence with you.
  • To deliver what you have paid for — course access, readings, downloads — and to provide support around it.
  • To tell you about new courses, offerings, or changes to something you are enrolled in.
  • To keep the website secure, working, and free from abuse.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We rely on your consent for marketing emails, on the performance of our agreement with you for delivering what you purchased, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the site for the rest.

04Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. We do use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

Wix Hosts the website, the members area, bookings, and payment links.
MailerLite Stores newsletter subscribers and contact-form submissions, and sends our emails.
Vercel Hosts the small service that passes newsletter and contact form submissions to MailerLite.
Payment processors Handle card and bank payments directly, under their own privacy terms.
Google Fonts Serves the typefaces used on this site; your browser requests them when a page loads.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect our rights or someone's safety.

05Cookies

This site uses cookies that are necessary for it to function — keeping you logged in to the members area, remembering your session, and keeping things secure. Our host also sets cookies that help us understand, in aggregate, how the site is being used.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the necessary ones, parts of the site — particularly member login and course access — will stop working.

06How Long We Keep It

  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe, and for a short period afterwards so we can honour your unsubscribe.
  • Enquiries and correspondence: for as long as needed to deal with the matter, and a reasonable period after.
  • Course and purchase records: for as long as you have access, plus the period our tax and accounting obligations require.
  • Birth details for readings: kept only as long as needed to deliver and follow up on the reading, unless you ask us to keep them for future sessions.

07Your Rights

You may ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, or delete it. You may withdraw consent for marketing at any time — every email we send carries an unsubscribe link, and it works immediately.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the GDPR, including the right to object to certain processing, to request a portable copy of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

To exercise any of this, write to info@shivohampath.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the period the law allows.

08Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections, and sensible access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we take the confidentiality of what you share with us seriously, particularly the personal matters that arise in readings and course work.

09International Transfers

Our service providers may store or process data on servers outside India, including in the European Union and the United States. Where that happens, we rely on those providers' contractual safeguards to protect your information.

10Children

This website and its offerings are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, write to us and we will delete it.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our offerings or our providers change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where a change materially affects your rights, we will say so directly.

QUESTIONS ABOUT YOUR PRIVACY

Write to info@shivohampath.com and we will answer you personally.
