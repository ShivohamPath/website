The Root Trika Shaivism · Kashmir · c. 850 CE Onwards

Kashmir Shaivism

The Non-Dual Tradition of the Trika

The philosophical and experiential lineage of non-dual Shaivism that emerged in Kashmir — asserting that Shiva is not a distant deity but the very consciousness within which all existence arises and dissolves. Its foundational texts — the Shiva Sutras, Spanda Karikas, Pratyabhijnahridayam, and the Vijnana Bhairava Tantra — map the direct path from human limitation to the recognition of one's nature as Shiva itself.

The Flame 14th Century · Kashmir · c. 1320 – 1392

Lalleshwari (Lal Ded)

The Only Woman Saint of the Trika Lineage · Wandering Yogini of Kashmir

Born into a Kashmiri Pandit family, initiated into Shaivism by her guru Siddha Srikantha, Lalleshwari renounced her household life and wandered naked through Kashmir — reciting Vakhs, mystic verses that are among the earliest compositions in the Kashmiri language. She was the only woman in the lineage of Trika Shaivism.

Her Vakhs carry no religion, no caste, no separation — only the direct experience of Shiva as the ground of all being. Kashmir has said for centuries: "We know only two words that are meaningful — one is Allah, the other is Lalla."

The Revival 19th Century · India · c. 1870 Onwards

Swami Gangadhar Tirth & Swami Narayan Dev Tirth

Revivalists of the Shaktipat Transmission

The science of Shaktipat — the direct transmission of awakening Shakti from Guru to disciple — has existed as an underground current in the Hindu spiritual tradition since ancient times. At the close of the 19th century, Swami Gangadhar Tirth brought it back into living form. A deeply reclusive yogi who rarely left his small cottage near Chandan Talab in Jagannathpuri, he initiated only one disciple — a young man from Bengal named Kali Kishore.

That disciple, who would come to be known as Swami Narayan Dev Tirth, was born in 1870 and initiated in 1889. Where his Guru had given the blessing to almost no one, Swami Narayan Dev Tirth gave it to many — traveling widely, writing, and heralding Shaktipat among the masses. It was he who restored this sacred science to the world.

The Transmission Contemporary · Siddha Maha Yoga Lineage

Guru Ji Greg Hagi

Shaktipat Guru · Seventh in an Unbroken Lineage from Swami Gangadhar Tirth

From Swami Gangadhar Tirth, the flame of Shaktipat passed without interruption through six masters before reaching Greg Hagi — each holding and transmitting the living Shakti to the next.

Greg Hagi spent over 30 years in sincere spiritual search — across physical yoga, shamanism, and many paths — driven by an inexhaustible longing for what he could feel but not yet name. Everything changed when he found his Guru, Col. T. Sreenivasulu (Swami Sahajananda Tirtha) — himself a disciple of the great Swami Shivom Tirth — and later received the personal blessing of Swami Sahajananda, a 98-year-old realised being. Initiated as a Shaktipat Guru into the Siddha Maha Yoga tradition, he now transmits this ancient awakening to sincere seekers worldwide.

You Are Here May 2024 · Shaktipat Initiation

Veena Maheshwari (Shivohampath)

Madhurya Bhakt · Initiated into the Lalleshwari Lineage

Born a Madhurya Bhakt — one who loves Shiva as Beloved rather than deity — Veena received formal initiation into the Lalleshwari lineage through Shaktipat from Guru Ji Greg Hagi in May 2024.

This was not a beginning. It was a recognition — a formalization of what her soul had been carrying lifetimes before this body was born.
