THE CONTAINER

Who This Is For

THIS COURSE IS FOR YOU IF
  • ✦You have tasted union — through She Who Becomes Him, or through your own devotion — and sensed there is something on the other side of it.
  • ✦You are ready for a tradition with real technical vocabulary: tattvas, malas, upāyas — studied precisely, not vaguely.
  • ✦You suspect the smallness you feel is not a personal failure but something structural — and you want to see through it.
  • ✦You want practice, not just philosophy: breath, mantra, body, thought — worked daily.
  • ✦You are willing to be asked what you caught yourself being, not just what you felt.
IT IS NOT FOR
  • ·Casual spiritual curiosity, or collecting another certificate.
  • ·Anyone looking for affirmations — bhāvanā is contemplation held until it becomes perception, and it asks more of you.
  • ·Those who want the comfort of distance kept intact. This course removes it.
  • ·Anyone unwilling to sit with a two-week pause where nothing is added and everything settles.
WHY THE CONTAINER IS CLOSED · WOMEN ONLY

Module 4 works directly with mātṛkā śakti — the power of the inner voice that names you — applied to what women specifically are told they are: about worth, body, desire, silence, and largeness. That work asks for a closed, protected space. This is not a course about women. It is a course held for them.
