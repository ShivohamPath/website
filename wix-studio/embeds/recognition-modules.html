THE JOURNEY

Seven Modules of Recognition

Each module moves through articles, practices and rituals, a lecture, a workbook, and self-inquiry — asking not what you felt, but what you caught yourself being.

I MODULE I · 7 STEPS

Caitanyam Ātmā

The Ground of Everything

The non-dual claim, established. A bridge from bhakti — and the dissolving of the first fear this course will raise: that recognition cancels devotion. It doesn't. It explains it.

  • 1ARTICLEYou Did Not Come Here to Find Him — pratyabhijñā as re-cognition, not attainment. Why this course begins where the last one ended.
  • 2ARTICLEDoes This End My Bhakti? — Utpaladeva, the tradition's greatest philosopher, wrote its most ecstatic devotional hymns. Non-dualism doesn't kill longing.
  • 3PRACTICEŚivo'ham Ajapa — the mantra already occurring in the breath. The practice you do not perform.
  • 4RITUALThe Unlit Lamp — sitting at the altar without invoking, without asking. A rite of not petitioning.
  • 5ARTICLEThe Map in One Sitting — the 36 tattvas for orientation, not memorisation. Where the descent happens, and where you are standing.
  • 6LECTURECaitanyam Ātmā: The First Sūtra
  • 7WORKBOOKWhat Have I Been Calling Distance? — with self-inquiry and doctrine check.
II MODULE II · 7 STEPS

The Forgetting

Malas and Kañcukas

The diagnostic — and perhaps the most immediately useful module in the course. You will recognise your own psychology in a thousand-year-old list.

  • 1ARTICLEĀṇava: The Wound of Feeling Small — the primal contraction. Not a flaw in you; the first move of manifestation.
  • 2ARTICLEMāyīya and Kārma — the sense of otherness, and the story of being the one who does.
  • 3PRACTICEThe Five Kañcukas Inventory — limited capacity, limited knowing, desire that never closes, dread of time, the feeling of being fated — mapped onto lived experience.
  • 4BODY PRACTICELocating the Contraction — where āṇava sits in the body. Breath into it, without trying to fix it.
  • 5ARTICLEThis Is Not Your Trauma — It Is the Structure of Manifestation — the reframe that depersonalises shame.
  • 6LECTUREThe Three Impurities and the Five Cloaks
  • 7WORKBOOKSeven-Day Contraction Log — with doctrine check.
III MODULE III · 7 STEPS · ĀṆAVOPĀYA

The Path Through the Body

Āṇavopāya — where technique begins

The Vijñāna Bhairava enters here, and remains the practice manual for the rest of the course.

  • 1ARTICLEThe Lowest Path Is Not the Lesser Path — the upāyas as a ladder of means, not of worth.
  • 2PRACTICEDvādaśānta: The Two Ends of Breath — the turning points where breath becomes still.
  • 3PRACTICEMadhya: Dwelling in the Centre — the pause; the central channel; the space that is always available.
  • 4PRACTICEUccāra and Mantra Vīrya — mantra as vibration, not repetition. The potency is in the utterance, not the count.
  • 5RITUALNyāsa: Placing the Divine in the Body — the body as temple, not vessel.
  • 6LECTUREThe Vijñāna Bhairava as a Working Manual
  • 7WORKBOOKPractice Log — with doctrine check.
IV MODULE IV · 7 STEPS · ŚĀKTOPĀYA

The Path Through the Mind

Śāktopāya — vikalpa work and mātṛkā śakti

The most psychologically potent module in the course — and the one where the women-only container justifies itself.

  • 1ARTICLEVikalpa: The Thought That Builds Your World — how construct becomes perception.
  • 2ARTICLEMātṛkā Śakti: The Goddess Hiding in Your Inner Voice — the voice that names you is itself a śakti. Unrecognised, and therefore running you.
  • 3PRACTICEŚuddha Vikalpa: Installing the True Thought — bhāvanā. Not affirmation: contemplation held until it becomes perception.
  • 4PRACTICEThe Gap Between Two Thoughts — a Vijñāna Bhairava dhāraṇā.
  • 5ARTICLEWhat Women Are Told They Are — mātṛkā applied to inherited beliefs about worth, body, desire, silence, and largeness. The heart of why this container is closed.
  • 6LECTURETurning the Mātṛkā
  • 7WORKBOOKVikalpa Audit — with doctrine check.
V MODULE V · 7 STEPS · ŚĀMBHAVOPĀYA

The Path of Will

Śāmbhavopāya — icchā, the flash, no method

Deliberately the shortest and starkest module. The format itself is the teaching.

  • 1ARTICLEThe Path With No Steps
  • 2PRACTICEBhairavī Mudrā — eyes open, gaze outward, awareness turned in. The world stays visible, and stops being other.
  • 3PRACTICEThe Instant Before Desire Takes an Object — icchā at its source, before it names what it wants.
  • 4PRACTICEDhāraṇās at the Edge of Intensity — the practices at the peak of fear, joy, sudden fright, longing, union. Approached only with a settled practice; intense states are never forced.
  • 5ARTICLEWhy This Cannot Be Practised, Only Received
  • 6LECTUREIcchā: The Will That Is Not Yours
  • 7WORKBOOKWhat Happened Without Me Doing It
TWO-WEEK INTEGRATION PAUSE The course stops here on purpose. So do you.
VI MODULE VI · 7 STEPS

Śaktipāta and Anupāya

Grace and the Pathless Path

The limits of effort — with Lalleshwari as the lineage's living proof: a woman's Shaivism, in the vernacular, in the body.

  • 1ARTICLEŚaktipāta: The Descent That Chooses You — the intensities of grace, from the overwhelming to the barely perceptible.
  • 2ARTICLELalleshwari: The Woman Who Walked Out — her vākhs. Not a mascot for this course; the proof of concept.
  • 3PRACTICESitting Without Method — anupāya.
  • 4RITUALThe Offering of Effort — releasing sādhana as achievement.
  • 5ARTICLE"Have I Received Grace?" Is the Wrong Question
  • 6LECTUREGrace, and the End of Earning
  • 7WORKBOOKLal Ded Contemplation — choose one vākh, and live inside it for seven days.
VII MODULE VII · 11 STEPS · THE CLOSE

Jīvanmukti: Living as Her

Integration — and the course's opposite of a wedding

The first course ended in an inner wedding. This one ends in something else entirely: not a marriage, but the end of anyone left to marry.

  • 1ARTICLEKramamudrā: When Inside and Outside Stop Being Two
  • 2ARTICLECamatkāra: Wonder as the Native Taste of Consciousness
  • 3PRACTICERecognition in Motion — the kitchen, the argument, the traffic, the marketplace. Where the course is actually tested.
  • 4ARTICLEThe Devotional Woman, Revisited — bhakti after recognition. Utpaladeva returns to close the circle opened in Module 1.
  • 5ARTICLESvātantrya: Freedom Was Always Her Own Name — not Shiva's attribute lent to Shakti. Her name.
  • 6CEREMONYThe Rite of Absolute Freedom — the closing ceremony.
  • 7WRITINGŚivo'ham, Written in My Own Hand
