Why this course exists

Parashara did not stop at the birth chart

Most Jyotish taught today stops at the birth chart and the Vimshottari dasha. Parashara did not. He gave sixteen divisions of the chart and named the deity who presides over each part of them. In his own scoring of those sixteen, the sixtieth part, the Shashtiamsa, carries more weight (4 out of 20) than the birth chart (3.5) or the Navamsa (3).

That part of the tradition is rarely taught, and there is a reason.

A division in the D60 lasts about two minutes of rising time. Read it on a birth time that is off by five minutes and you are reading someone else's chart.

So this course begins where the serious work begins: with the time of birth. You will learn to rectify a birth time, defend it, and grade how much it can bear. Only then do you open the fine divisions, the deities and the weight of the past.

Who this is for

You can already read a chart

Signs, houses, planets, nakshatras and the Vimshottari dasha. Foundations of Jyothish is one way to get there, but it is not required. Module 0 is a bridge that teaches the calculations by hand, so everyone starts level.

This is for you if

You want to derive things from a chart yourself and know which text stands behind each claim.

It is not for you if

You want quick predictions, or readings handed to you. No fine division is read on an unrectified time. Past-life findings are never delivered as verdicts.

Questions

Before you enrol

Do I need to have done Foundations of Jyothish?

No. You need to be able to read a chart. Module 0 brings everyone to the same footing on calculation. If you have not read a chart before, take Foundations first, or take the bundle.

I don't know my exact birth time. Can I still join?

Yes. The enrolment form asks how your birth time was recorded, whether from a hospital record, a parent's memory or not at all. Modules 1 and 2 teach you to test it. The D60 and the nadiamsa are only read on a time graded fit for them.

What if I miss a class?

The recording and notes are on Wix the same evening.

Will my chart be shown to the group?

Not without your consent. The capstone uses a chart from a peer who has agreed to it.

Is this predictive astrology?

It teaches technique: how to fix a birth time, read the fine divisions and test a reading against a life. It does not hand out verdicts. Past-life findings are written up only where independent methods agree, and Karmavipaka material is taught on historical charts only.

Is this Shaiva astrology?

It is classical Jyotish, taught from named texts. Where I bring a Shaiva understanding, it is labelled as mine and kept apart from what the text says.

What is the refund policy?

You get a full refund if you withdraw before Module 1 begins on 23 January. There are no refunds after that.
