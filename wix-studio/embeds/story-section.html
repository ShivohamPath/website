01 THE
SEARCHING

A Soul That Could Not Fit.

I grew up knowing something was off — not with me, but with the map everyone else was following. The ordinary milestones felt borrowed. The ordinary gods felt distant. But Shiva — even His name carried a pull I could not explain. Not the Shiva of textbooks. The Shiva of silence. Of destruction that loves.

I searched everywhere that felt alive: temples, texts, teachers, lineages. I was looking for something I could not name but would absolutely recognise the moment I found it. What I did not know then is that the searching was itself the preparation.

02 THE
INITIATION

When the Forgotten River Began Flowing Again.

Shaktipat did not arrive gently. It arrived the way a locked door finally gives — all at once, with the weight of everything that had been pressing against it. In that transmission, I did not receive new knowledge. I remembered what I already was. The river had always been there; it had simply been underground.

What followed were years of sadhana — not the kind that is scheduled and tidy, but the kind that restructures you from the inside. Kashmiri Shaivism became not a philosophy I studied but a reality I inhabited. Jyotish became a language through which I could read karma the way one reads footprints — backwards and forwards across many lifetimes.

03 THE
OFFERING

Why Shivoham Path Was Born.

I kept meeting the same soul in different bodies. Seekers who had tried every doorway — therapy, religion, self-help — and still felt the ache. Not the ache of something broken, but the ache of something holy that had not yet been met.

They did not need more information. They needed someone who could read the karmic architecture beneath their life — and hold the space while they finally recognised themselves in it. That is what a D60 reading does. That is what this path does.

Shivoham Path is not a brand. It is a response. Every reading, every course, every word written here carries one intention: to be a clear mirror for souls who are already on their way home — but have forgotten which direction that is.

Because in the end, we do not find Shiv. He finds us. And when He does, our entire life becomes His path.
