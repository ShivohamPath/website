Not everyone is ready for this path. And that is perfectly correct. This course is not for the spiritually curious — it is for the spiritually committed.

✦ Women who feel Shiv not as a deity but as a presence — an inexplicable intimacy that has no rational explanation in this lifetime.
✦ Seekers who have felt the dark night — the silence, the spiritual longing that feels like grief, the ache for something this world cannot give.
✦ Sacred rebels done with surface spirituality — who need depth, honesty, and a guide who will not soften the truth to make it comfortable.
✦ Those who sense their devotion is ancient — older than this life, older than this body, written somewhere in the soul's oldest memory.
